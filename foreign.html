Purpose of Data Collection

To verify the identity of visitors and contractors before entering the site, in place of physically holding your passport at the security checkpoint, and to enable investigation in case of a security incident on EGAT premises.

Data Collected

  • Full name, company/organization, phone number
  • Passport number / national ID number
  • Facial photograph
  • Vehicle plate and type (if applicable)
  • Date and time of entry

Retention Period

Your data will be retained for no more than 90 days from the date of registration, after which it will be deleted, unless retention is required for an ongoing investigation.

Data Controller

Electricity Generating Authority of Thailand (EGAT), Nong Chok Office, Tel. 2205

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) B.E. 2562, by contacting the data controller above.
